### Hi, I'm Jose Maria 👋

I build **AI agents and automations for real business processes** at
[Nomad Prompters](https://nomadprompters.es): booking assistants, support bots and
integrations that connect LLMs with the tools a business already uses (n8n, PostgreSQL, Gmail,
calendars, Telegram).

I focus on getting the plumbing right: business rules in the database rather than the prompt,
human hand-off when it matters, privacy by design and regression tests for agent behaviour.
